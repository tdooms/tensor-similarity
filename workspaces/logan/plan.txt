GOAL:
  Train a 1-layer bilinear attention-only model on SimpleStories (vocab ~4k),
  save checkpoints, then measure: what fraction of the attention tensor's norm
  lives on the diagonal of the attention pattern?

  Metric = ||T_diag||^2 / ||T_causal||^2
  where T_diag restricts the attention to diagonal (t == s) only.

STEP 2: MODEL — 1-layer bilinear attention-only  [DONE]
================================================================================
- model.py: AttnOnlyModel wrapping src/components/attention.py
- Architecture: Embed -> Attention -> RMSNorm -> Unembed (tied weights)

  KEY DESIGN DECISIONS (per Thomas' recommendations + team chat):
  - NO RMSNorm inside attention: self.attn.norm = nn.Identity()
    Easier to analyze as tensor. The QK normalization is what gets "ugly"
    for tensor decomposition.
  - RMSNorm PRE-UNEMBED only: stabilizes training without complicating
    the attention tensor structure.
  - Scaling: d_head^2 (not sqrt) already in Attention class
  - Residual lerp: scale=0.5 = 1/(2*sqrt(1 layer))
  - No MLP.

  Hyperparams: d_model=64, n_head=4, d_head=16, n_ctx=128
  Params: 286,784 (~287k)
  Tested: forward pass OK on random input.

STEP 4: NORM COMPUTATION  [DONE — MC approach]
================================================================================
- tensorize.py: Monte Carlo norm estimation

  IMPORTANT MATH NOTES (documented in tensorize.py):
  The attention tensor T maps (x_t, x_s) -> y_t where:
    y_t = scale/d_head^2 * sum_h M(t,s) * <R_t Q1_h, x_t> * <R_t Q2_h, x_t> *
                                            <R_s K1_h, x_s> * <R_s K2_h, x_s> *
                                            O_h V_h x_s

  T is degree 2 in x_t (Q1, Q2 share it) and degree 3 in x_s (K1, K2, V share it).
  Total degree 5.

  The exact Frobenius norm ||T||^2 requires computing 4th-order moments for x_t
  and 6th-order moments for x_s (Isserlis/Wick theorem). This is what Thomas'
  isserlis.py infrastructure handles for the MLP case.

  FOR NOW: Monte Carlo estimation — feed random Gaussian x through the attention
  path (with different masks) and measure output variance. This gives the
  "operational" norm and the ratio ||T_diag||^2 / ||T_causal||^2 is still valid.

  Martin is implementing the exact TN contraction for bilinear attention. We can
  swap in the exact method later.

  INIT SANITY CHECK (n_samples=64):
    norm_causal:   1.54e-07
    norm_diag:     3.01e-09
    diag_fraction: 0.0195
    Expected naive: 1/64.5 ≈ 0.0155 (diagonal is 1/avg_ctx positions in causal)
    Close enough — at random init the attention is roughly uniform, so diagonal
    fraction ≈ fraction of mask entries that are diagonal.

DIAGONAL FRACTION ANALYSIS:
  - Init (step 0):    diag_frac = 0.0086  (theory: ~1/128.5 = 0.0078 for uniform causal)
  - Step 1500:        diag_frac = 0.68    (!!!) — massive spike, model suddenly learns diagonal
  - Step 2000:        diag_frac = 0.39    — rapid decay from spike
  - Step 3000+:       diag_frac stabilizes around 0.20-0.25, noisy
  - Final (step 37002): diag_frac = 0.20

  INTERPRETATION:
  1. At init, attention is approximately uniform -> diagonal gets ~1/T_avg share.
  2. Very early in training (step ~1500), the model discovers "copy" (diagonal)
     attention as a useful circuit. The spike to 0.68 means 68% of the tensor's
     energy is concentrated on self-attention (token attending to itself).
  3. As training continues, the model develops richer cross-token attention
     patterns, and the diagonal fraction drops and stabilizes at ~0.20-0.25.
     This is still 25-30x higher than the init value (~0.009), meaning the
     diagonal remains overrepresented relative to random/uniform attention.
  4. Both total norms grow enormously (~1e-7 to ~1e8, 15 orders of magnitude)
     during training — the attention tensor becomes much "stronger" overall.
  5. The off-diagonal norm also grows, just slightly slower than diagonal.

  The noisy oscillation (~0.18-0.26) in the stable regime may indicate:
  - MC variance (128 samples, could increase)
  - Actual fluctuation in attention structure during training
  - A longer run (3 epochs) might show whether this tightens up.
